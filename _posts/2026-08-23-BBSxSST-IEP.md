The story started three weeks ago, on 3rd August. The bus of Indonesian exchange students had just arrived in SST. Everyone was eager to meet their buddies, weeks of preparation and bfiefing led up to the next few days. Over the course of 3 days, we attended STEAM workshops on electronics, environmental chemistry, design, etc. We also went through classroom immersions together. My BBS teammates were Chendarrent and Grayson, who were really fun to work with and be around. 

3 Days seemed to fly in seconds as it was time to send them off. On wednesday, we concluded with a grand closing ceromony, leading them down to the Green Wall to take one final photo with them before they leave SST in their 3-day immersion. I am quite impressed, that we managed to grow so close in just a few days, from mere strangers to close friends. 

Of course, they didn't come to Singapore purely for fun. The problem statement posed to the 9 PIK groups and the 5 BDG (BBS Bandung) groups was "Breathe Local, Think Global", which saw groups creating a device to address the worsening air quality in Singapore and Indonesia. In those short 3 days, we worked on chemistry of air filters, aerodynamics, comupter-assisted design (CAD) and more.

# Jakarta: 
## Day 1

Two weeks later, on Sunday, 16 August, the contingent of 52 SST students left for Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta Airport (CGK) on SQ956. Waking up at 430am was no issue for most of us as we were too excited to sleep anyway. On the first day, we landed in Jakarta and met our local tour guides, and saw a visit to a traditional batik musuem where we had the opportunity to create our own batik patterns on a piece of fabric. Then, we proceeded to a cultural walk before a historical museum tour in Kota Bambu Selatan, West Jakarta. This just so happened to be in the middle of their Independence Day celebrations, so admist the chaos of the raves and partying, we gained a rare insight into how different countries celebrate their holidays. We finally checked in to our hotel, Hilton Garden, to stay for the night. 

## Day 2

Monday started with a hearty western breakfast from the hotel buffet. We first visited a hydroponics farm in South Jakarta, entering their massive greenhouse where we even had a hands-on experience on harvesting our own lettuce! After that, it was unfortunately time to say goodbye to the BDG group as they had a high-speed train to catch which would bring them to Bandung. Before lunch, we went for a short Mangrove tour, immersing ourselves in the rich biodiversity of Indonesia. Lunch, however, was not so straightforward. 

![My classmates talking.](/assets/images/bbs-pik-2026/talk.jpg)

Sitting in the 160+ PM2.5 AQI was about 50 packed boxes, with no clear information of when they were cooked or how long they've been out in the open. The restaurant that seemed to provide the lunches could not guarantee it's safety. Thus, we spent 30-45 minutes watching the teachers pace around hastily, making dozens of calls, with no idea of what was happening in the moment. After that, we finally boarded the bus again, leaving for the mall beneath our hotel, Green Sedayu Mall, with lunch money to purchase our own lunch. 

What really happened? Well, the tour agency changed our itinerary last minute without informing the teachers. In fact, we were supposed to have lunch with the Bandung group. The sudden change of plans resulted in a craze of last-minute actions. Fortunately, our teachers acted fast, bringing us back to Green Sedayu Mall for a free-roam lunch. 

## Day 3 

Tuesday was the first day we arrived in BBS PIK! Although we woke up at 5am, all of us were half-dead and excited to meet our buddies at the same time. We started off the day with an immersion of their Independence Day. We then went througha school tour before we were given time to work on our air purifier prototypes. Find out more about it [here](https://gnnadia123.github.io/).

## Day 4

Preliminaries have started! After a few hours of data collection and consolidation, we prepared a presentation to convince our judges that our air purifier was worthy of a spot in the finals. The presentation went smoothly, everyone played their part well and we performed decently. 

As we gathered in the Auditorium for the reveal of the finalist teams, we held our breath as we heard "PIK4 will be going to finals". A myraid of emotions flooded us at the same time - excitement, fear, etc. This was huge - an oppurtunity to prove to the cohort that we had the best air purifier amongst the 3 finalist teams, 1, 4 (us), and 7. 

## Day 5

**D-Day.**

I woke up at a ripe 430am, being unable to sleep. Over the past 2 days all of us got less than 10 hours of sleep, and even so we knew we had to sacrifice our sleep to get our presentation to top-notch. The bus ride was not any better - even a 40-minute ride allowed us to get online and make final touches. We revamped our presentation from a mere monotonous script to a fun and engaging skit.

Presenting in front of the entire secondary 1, 2, 3, and SST IDP cohort was nerve-wracking. Although I had spoke in front of the S3 cohort during [Student Congress](https://gnnadia123.github.io/2026/07/09/S3-Student-Congress.html), 300+ pairs of eyes across two different countries listening to your presentation was something different. 

After it was all over, we heaved a sigh of relief. The hardest part of the entire trip was over. Before we knew it, we were already gathered back in the Auditorium for the prize giving. The three finalist teams all stood a chance to win one of three awards: 
1. **Most Creative Design** - A team whose design choices stood out in terms of creativeness while still having outstanding functionality. 
2. **Good For People Award** - A team whose design and philosophy was designed with people-centered benefits.
3. **Deisgn Excellence Award** - The team which showcased the overall best and strongest design concept in all areas. 

We did not expect to get our hopes up, but we were shocked and pleasantly surprised to receive the Design Excellence Award! The sleepless nights over the past 3 days were worth every second. We were all incredibly honoured to be standing on stage receiving the most prestigous prize of the whole competition. 

## Day 6

Before we left, we took a visit to Apple Developer Center PIK, where we learnt about their developer program and how they support up-and-coming developers. 

![The majestic skyline view from the Building.](/assets/images/bbs-pik-2026/viewfromapple.JPG)

It's been very fun in Jakarta. Meeting new people, immersing myself in their culture, the list could go on forever. I'm truly grateful for such a wonderful group, and amazing teachers who made everything flow so brilliantly and smoothly even in uncertain times.

After all, we learnt that winning wasn't really what we were happy about. Making new friends, bonding over shared meals, struggling together on a problem, those strike a chord deep within me - deeper than any certificate, medal or trophy could give. Chendarrent and Grayson were the best group mates I could ever ask for. While there were areas for improvement, it's the wabi-sabi in everything that makes something truly memorable. I wouldn't have it any other way. 

Thanks for everything, BBS - I'm already missing the wonderful people and culture Jakarta has to offer. See you guys down the road!
