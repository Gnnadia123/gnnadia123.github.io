On 5th and 6th November, I took part in Hack N' Flag 2026, a student run cybersecurity capture-the-flag competition by Ngee Ann Polytechnic's Cybersecurity Special Interest Group. Over the course of 2 days, I took part with teammates Kenji, Timothy, and Makx, in team SSTetrahedron. 

While this was ongoing, I was feeling under the weather, so I did not attend the concurrently running IDP eXtended Learning Experience, but I made use of the time at home to start on the challenges. This was my first CTF, which is my stepping stone into cybersecurity, trying new things out to find hidden interests I might have. 

Stepping into the world of cybersecurity was no easy feat. Especially right after the [SAS Spooky Robo Rumble](https://gnnadia123.github.io/2026/11/02/SAS-Spooky-Robo-Rumble.html), I had to cram as much CTF knowledge as possible before the competition. Overall, I managed to solve a total of 9 challenges, spanning multiple categories including Web, OSINT, Pwn, and more. 

Unfortunately, we qualified as the 33rd ranked team, which did not progress us to the in-person finals. However, it was still an amazing experience which allowed me to learn more about cybersecurity.
