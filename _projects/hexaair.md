# HexaAir: The future of air purifiers

What started as a simple BBS x SST exchange resulted in a modular, personal air cleansing device. 

Air quality should never be a luxury, nor should it require a technical manual to achieve. We created HexaAir to bridge the gap between high-performance air purification and universal accessibility. Driven by the core design statement that everyone deserves clean air, the goal was to develop an intuitive, "plug-and-play" device that anyone could operate immediately. HexaAir removes the friction from environmental wellness, transforming a complex appliance into a seamless, welcoming addition to the modern home.

## Our Design Phiosophy

HexaAir revolves around 3 core pillars: modularity, simplicity of operation, and portability. 

### 1. Modularity 

The defining characteristic of HexaAir is its distinct hexagonal shape, a deliberate aesthetic choice deeply rooted in our foundational pillar of modularity. Inspired by nature’s engineering marvels - the bees - most efficient packing structure, the geometry allows multiple HexaAir units to seamlessly tile, stack, or link together. This geometric harmony ensures that the system can effortlessly scale alongside a user’s evolving living or office space, proving that industrial design can be both highly functional and visually striking. 

### 2. Simplicity Of Operation

At the heart of HexaAir is a commitment to radical simplicity, stripping away the dense menus and frustrating setups within traditional smart appliances. We wanted a true "plug-and-play" design, ensuring that the device requires zero technical expertise, application pairing, or complex configuration to run. Once plugged in, the system instantly takes over—using its automated sensors and intelligent fan to quietly manage air quality in the background. This frictionless user experience ensures that clean, healthy air is immediately accessible to everyone, from young children to tech-averse seniors, the moment it is turned on.

### 3. Portability 

Our third pillar is portability, which challenges the traditional air purifier model. Instead of buying a massive, expensive machine to scrub an entire room, HexaAir focuses strictly on the user's immediate breathing zone. Because it only requires enough power to clean the air directly around you, it drastically saves on material and energy costs. It doesn't matter if you're in a tiny cubicle or a massive warehouse - HexaAir cleans your air, and your air only.

## Prototyping

### Version 1

Our first design consisted of three hexagons, one to house electronics and two to house the filter itself. In theory, this sounds good, but we ran into a load of problems. First of all, the hexagon housing electronics were difficult to lock into place as there was no effective way to lock them together. Having three hexagons also made the promise of portability redundant, as it was quite bulky. Last, the connection between both hexagons affected airflow, resulting in a poor fan efficiency.

### Version 2

With that in mind, we started to ideate on version two, which saw the hexagon for electronics removed, along with an added pipe. This reduces the bulkiness of our prototype, making it more portable and easier to construct. The pipe was placed with cleaner airflow in mind. 

However, we ran into a few problems again. Firstly, designing the air filters to accommodate the air pipe’s circular shape posed many difficulties, as there was no easy way to get sufficient interlocking friction. Secondly, it would be difficult to maintain airtightness in the pipe and to prevent backflow after traveling such a long distance, rendering the pipe useless. Thirdly, since the fan is on a regular frame, there might be gaps between the fan and the tunnel which allows air to leak, affecting the airflow and reducing the efficiency of our air purifier.

### Final Design

HexaAir's final prototype consisted of just one singular hexagon, a nod to the original modulairty we had in mind as a design choice. It's sleek, compact, and completely built around the core geometric shape of a hexagon. Looking closely at the unit, you'll see our key considerations brought to life. First is the hexagonal chassis itself. This simple design allows for portability and ease of transport, allowing users to carry it around to different rooms they go to. The single hexagon shape ensures there are minimal air leakages, maximising overall airflow efficiency. Secondly, we included interchangeable filter layers that easily slot and click into place. These filter plates are the highlight of modularity in the final prototype, trapping harmful pollutants such as PM2.5 and VOCs. The design makes it very convenient to replace the air filters once they get worn out, just slide the old filter plate out, and slot the new filter plate in. Thirdly, we integrated real-time air quality sensors linked directly to a built-in display, giving the user live feedback on the air they are breathing.

### Filter Materials

![Our filter](/assets/images/bbs-pik-2026/bbsfilter.JPG)

We tested a few specific materials. Filter A utilizes a standard HEPA filter as a control variable, which is the industry standard for filtration of extremely small airborne particles such as PM2.5. 

For Filter B, we used Meltblown Polypropylene. This is a fine-fiber, nonwoven material that you'd commonly find in face masks. It has fantastic filtering properties, similar to the HEPA filter. Since they are both designed for trapping PM2.5, we decided to use KN95 face masks as a replacement HEPA filter. 

We also used an activated carbon filter which filters out VOCs from the air. This filter was a fixed constant in each experiment, whereas Filter A and B were swapped between experiments to test their effectiveness in reducing the concentration of pollutants in the air.

## Verdict

Based on the data, we found that the HEPA filter performed better for improving the air quality when put in our air purifier. It was able to reduce particulate matter by 34.1% in just 4 minutes compared to the KN95 alternative which reduced it by 15.4% in the same time. But given that our alternative filter would be much cheaper than a HEPA filter, our alternative filter still serves its purpose as a more suitable alternative for our air purifier. This is because it is still able to perform at a decently comparable effectiveness at filtering PM2.5, while being a fraction of the cost of an actual HEPA filter, making it more accessible to budget-conscious households.

If you look at the current market, commercial air purifies force families to spend hundreds. They draw heavy amounts of electricity to clean entire rooms, and require proprietary HEPA filters that cost a fortune to replace. HexaAir flips this model to support budget-conscious households.

Because HexaAir targets the immediate breathing zone like a study desk or a bedside table, and runs on minimal power, it keeps monthly electricity bills untouched. More importantly, our modular design utilises standard KN95 material. This drops the HEPA filter maintenance cost from tens of dollars down to just a few. HexaAir provides a highly affordable clean air zone that anyone can easily maintain. Furthermore, since it targets your immediate breathing area, you’ll feel the effects much sooner.

## Results

After everything, we managed to clinch the **Design Excellence Award**, the certification of highest honour and merit out of all nine PIK teams. The award recognises an "overall excellent prototype, problem statement, and design philosophy". We are incredibly honoured to receive this and would like to thank everyone for giving us this opportunity!

Designing HexaAir challenged us to balance complex environmental engineering with radical user simplicity. Stripping away unnecessary interfaces to achieve a true "plug-and-play" experience required rigorous iteration on both the internal airflow layout and the external form factor. Ultimately, HexaAir stands as a testament to our design philosophy: that truly impactful technology doesn't demand the user's attention—it quietly elevates their quality of life.
